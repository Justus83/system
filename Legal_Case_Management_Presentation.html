⚖️ LEGAL CASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Complete Presentation - How It Works

🎯 What Is This System?

A web-based platform that connects lawyers and clients online:

👨‍⚖️ For Lawyers:
Register, accept cases, manage clients, get paid online
👤 For Clients:
Find lawyers, request cases, make payments, communicate
🔒 For Security:
Admin approval, encrypted payments, data protection
📱 For Access:
Works on desktop, mobile, tablet - any browser

📌 Step-by-Step Workflow

STEP 1: Registration & Login

Lawyer Registration Process:

1. Click "Register as Lawyer"
2. Enter: Name, Email, Password
3. Select: Legal Specialization (Family Law, Criminal, Corporate, etc.)
4. Enter: Office Name, Address, Phone, Aadhar ID
5. Upload: Professional Photo
6. WAIT for Admin Approval ⏳
7. Once Approved → Can Login & Accept Cases ✅

Client Registration Process:

1. Click "Register as Client"
2. Enter: Name, Email, Password
3. Enter: Full Address, Phone, Aadhar ID
4. Upload: Photo
5. INSTANT APPROVAL ✅
6. Can Login Immediately & Request Cases

📋 Step 2: Client Submits a Case

Location: Client Dashboard
Action: Click "Request Case Help"
Fill In:
• Case Type (Family Law, Criminal, etc.)
• Description of Problem
• Select Desired Lawyer from List
Status: 🔴 PENDING (Waiting for Lawyer to Review)

✅ Step 3: Lawyer Reviews & Approves Case

Location: Lawyer Dashboard
Notification: Lawyer receives alert about new case
Review: Click case to view full details
Decision: Two Options
    ✅ APPROVE Case
    ❌ REJECT Case
Result:
    If APPROVED → Status Changes to 🟠 APPROVED
    If REJECTED → Status Changes to 🔴 REJECTED

💰 Step 4: Client Confirms & Makes Payment

Notification: Client gets alert that lawyer approved case ✅
View: Case Status changes to APPROVED
Invoice: Amount Due is displayed (e.g., $500)
Action: Click "Pay Now" Button
Payment: Enter Card Details & Confirm
Result: Status Changes to 🟢 PAID
Next: CASE BECOMES ACTIVE 🎯

💬 Step 5: Lawyer & Client Communicate

How Client Sends Messages:

• Go to "My Cases" in Client Dashboard
• Select the Active Case
• Click "Send Message to Lawyer"
• Type Your Message
• Click SEND ✉️
• Message appears in Chat History

How Lawyer Replies:

• Go to "Case Messages" in Lawyer Dashboard
• See Client's Message
• Click "Reply"
• Type Response
• Click SEND
• Client Gets Notification & Sees Reply

📊 Step 6: Track Invoices & Payments

What Lawyer Sees:

• Go to "Finance" Section in Dashboard
• View All Cases with:
    → Case Name
    → Amount Charged
    → Payment Status (Paid/Unpaid)
    → Payment Date
• Download Invoice PDFs
• Generate Income Reports

What Client Sees:

• Go to "My Invoices" Section
• View All Invoices:
    → Case Name
    → Amount Due
    → Payment Status (Paid/Unpaid)
    → Due Date
• Click "Pay Now" for Unpaid Invoices
• Download Invoice PDFs for Records

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I register?
A: Click "Register as Client" or "Register as Lawyer", fill in the required information, and submit. Clients get instant access. Lawyers wait for admin approval.
Q: How long does lawyer approval take?
A: Usually 24-48 hours. The admin team reviews credentials and approves qualified lawyers.
Q: Can I change lawyers after requesting a case?
A: Yes, you can cancel the current case request and submit a new one with a different lawyer.
Q: Is payment safe and secure?
A: Yes! We use encrypted payment gateways that protect your card details. Your information is never stored unsecurely.
Q: Can lawyers see my personal information?
A: Lawyers only see the information you provide for your case. Your personal details are kept private.
Q: What if a lawyer doesn't respond?
A: Contact the admin team for mediation. We monitor cases and can intervene if needed.
Q: Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?
A: Refund policies depend on the case status. Contact admin for dispute resolution.
Q: How do I download invoices?
A: Go to your "Invoices" section and click "Download PDF" on any invoice.
